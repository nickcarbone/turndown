The programme

Everything Turndown can play. A glowing mark means it's live and in its hour now. Tap any line to switch to it.

Live streams come and go. If one fails or goes quiet for fifteen seconds, Turndown moves on and greys it out until tomorrow. On iPhone the timer stops the sound at the end; iOS won't let a web page fade a live stream's volume. Times are shown in Eastern time and follow each place's clocks, including daylight saving.
